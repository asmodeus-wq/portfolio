CHAPTER 03

My Process

A personal journey from understanding people to shipping thoughtful, impactful experiences.

Over the years, I’ve developed a rhythm that helps me move from ambiguity to clarity. It’s not a rigid checklist — it’s a living process that adapts to the project, the team, and the people we’re designing for.

THE JOURNEY

How I Work

Scroll down to move through the process horizontally

01

Discover

I start by stepping into the users’ world. Interviews, contextual inquiries, analytics deep-dives, and stakeholder conversations help me uncover the real problems beneath the surface.

User interviews & observation
Business goals alignment
Current state analysis
“The best solutions begin with genuine curiosity.”
02

Define

Raw insights are turned into clarity. I synthesize research into personas, journey maps, and a sharp problem statement that everyone on the team can rally around.

Affinity mapping & synthesis
Personas & journey maps
Clear problem framing
“A well-defined problem is already half-solved.”
03

Explore

This is where creativity explodes. I generate dozens of ideas — from wild concepts to practical solutions — then narrow them down through quick sketches and low-fidelity explorations.

Crazy 8s & sketching
Concept workshops
Divergent thinking sessions
“Quantity leads to quality.”
04

Craft

Selected ideas are brought to life. I design high-fidelity interfaces, build interactive prototypes, and pay close attention to micro-interactions, accessibility, and visual polish.

High-fidelity design
Interactive prototypes
Design systems & components
“Details are not details. They make the design.”
05

Evolve

Design is never finished. I test with real users, gather feedback, measure impact, and continuously refine. The best products evolve long after launch.

Usability testing
Analytics & feedback loops
Post-launch iteration
“Ship, learn, improve, repeat.”

This process is alive.
