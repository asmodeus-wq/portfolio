THE APPROACH

Not a rigid
checklist.

Over 8 years, I’ve developed a flexible rhythm that moves from ambiguity to clarity. It adapts to the project, the team, and most importantly — the people we’re designing for.

This process isn’t linear. It loops, overlaps, and evolves. But these five stages form the backbone of how I work — connected by one continuous journey.

01
STAGE ONE

Discover

I step into the users’ world to uncover the real problems beneath the surface.

User interviews & observation
Contextual inquiries
Analytics deep dives
Stakeholder alignment
02
STAGE TWO

Define

Raw insights become sharp clarity. I turn research into a problem everyone can rally around.

Affinity mapping & synthesis
Personas & journey maps
Problem framing workshops
Opportunity identification
03
STAGE THREE

Explore

Creativity explodes here. I generate dozens of ideas before narrowing down to the strongest directions.

Crazy 8s sketching sessions
Concept workshops
Low-fidelity wireframing
Divergent thinking
04
STAGE FOUR

Craft

Selected directions are refined into high-fidelity designs and interactive prototypes with careful attention to detail.

High-fidelity interface design
Interactive prototyping
Micro-interactions & motion
Accessibility & polish
05
STAGE FIVE

Evolve

Design never truly ends. I test, measure, learn, and continuously improve long after launch.

Usability testing & feedback
Analytics & behavior tracking
Post-launch iteration
Continuous improvement loops

This process is alive.

It flexes based on constraints, team size, and complexity. The goal stays constant: create experiences that feel obvious, delightful, and deeply human.
